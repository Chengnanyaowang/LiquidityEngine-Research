# Product roadmap

The public reference repository tracks the reusable research-infrastructure layer. The commercial product develops privately with design partners.

## Current public reference

- Synthetic market-state and evidence contracts.
- Deterministic research permission gate.
- Decision-record digest and replay demonstration.
- Explicit IP, security, and data-redaction boundaries.

## Next public milestones

1. Versioned decision-record schemas and migration examples.
2. A browser-based synthetic replay viewer.
3. Additional public fixtures covering incomplete data and event-risk workflows.
4. A public evaluation harness for contract and replay reliability.

## Product direction

LiquidityEngine begins with BTC perpetual-market research and aims to support professional digital-asset teams that need a more disciplined bridge between live data, internal methods, AI interpretation, operational controls, and post-decision learning.

Future commercial work may include private workspaces, strategy-migration tooling, governed replay archives, team permissions, data partnerships, and research APIs. These are product directions, not claims that such features are already available.
